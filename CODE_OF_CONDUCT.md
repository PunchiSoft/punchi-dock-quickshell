# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in this project is open to anyone who treats other people with
respect. We want bug reports, reviews and design discussions to stay about the
work, in English, and usable by whoever reads them later.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific and technical. Describe the behavior, the environment and the
  evidence instead of judging the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask a clarifying question before escalating a
  disagreement.
- Respect the project's safety contract: keep Plasma running, do not claim
  capabilities that were not validated, and never publish someone's private
  paths, logs or credentials.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution whose scope, evidence or
  maintenance cost does not fit the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments about a person or a
  group.
- Sexualized language or attention, and unwelcome contact of any kind.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing issues, reviewing the contributor instead of the code,
  or repeating demands after a maintainer has answered.
- Security disclosures in public channels.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and any other
channel used to work on this repository.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the GitHub profile linked in
the repository owner name (`PunchiSoft`). Include what happened, where and what
outcome you need; do not post it publicly. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close a thread, reject a
contribution, or block an account. The response is proportional to the behavior
and to whether it repeats. A maintainer involved in the incident delegates the
decision when another maintainer is available.

## Attribution

This policy follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
without reproducing its text.
